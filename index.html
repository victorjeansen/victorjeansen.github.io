November 2026 Quantitative Risk

Corporate FX Hedging & Monte Carlo Risk Engine — EUR/USD Margin Sensitivity

Personal project. Monte Carlo simulation of 12-month operating margin distributions for a hypothetical European exporter with USD revenues ($100M USD sales vs. €75M EUR fixed costs). Calibrates a zero-cost option collar (1.0745–1.1200) using Garman-Kohlhagen pricing and Brent's root-finding over 10,000 GBM paths. In this scenario, the hedge sets a €14.29M EBIT floor, above a €12.0M bank covenant.
